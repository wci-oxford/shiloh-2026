Quick answers

For first-timers and friends

Do I need a ticket?
No. Shiloh is free and open to everyone.
Do I need to register?
No. Just come. Request a pickup below if you need a ride to the venue.
Where is it happening?
Shiloh is broadcast from Canaanland. The Prayer Hour, Morning Sessions and Specialised Classes are online. The Saturday 6:00am Impartation Service and the evening Encounter Nights are in person at St Mary and St John CE Primary School, Iffley Road, Oxford.
Do I come every day?
Yes, if you can. Six days, every session. Each day builds on the one before.

Before you come

What it is, when, where, arriving

What is Shiloh?Since 1999. Why the whole Winners family stops for it

Shiloh is the annual prophetic gathering of Living Faith Church Worldwide (Winners’ Chapel), instituted in 1999 through Bishop David Oyedepo. Every December the Winners family gathers at Canaanland, Ota, and in viewing centres across the nations to hear the Word, pray and receive what God has for the coming year.

Shiloh 2026 runs Tuesday 8 to Sunday 13 December under the theme Far Above.

Come for all of it

Plan your week around it and come expecting. Everyone is welcome: members, first-timers, friends and family. Think of one person who needs this and bring them along.

What happens when I arrive?Welcome desk, first-timers, what to bring

Come to the welcome desk at the entrance if it is your first time. Someone will show you where to sit and where everything is. Arrive before the session starts and stay to the end.

What to bring

Yourself first, and someone with you. Then your Bible, notebook and pen, a warm layer for the early-morning sessions, and your offering.
