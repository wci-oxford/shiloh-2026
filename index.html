Quick answers

For first-timers and friends

Do I need a ticket?

No. Shiloh is free and open to everyone.

Do I need to register?

No. Just come. Request a pickup below if you need a ride to the venue.

Where is it happening?

Shiloh is broadcast from Canaanland. The Prayer Hour, Morning Sessions and Specialised Classes are online. The Saturday 5:00am Impartation Service and the evening Encounter Nights are in person at St Mary and St John CE Primary School, Iffley Road, Oxford.

What is Shiloh?

Shiloh is the annual prophetic gathering of Living Faith Church Worldwide (Winners’ Chapel), instituted in 1999 through Bishop David Oyedepo. Every December the Winners family gathers at Canaanland, Ota, and in viewing centres across the nations to hear the Word, pray and receive what God has for the coming year.

Do I come every day?

Yes, if you can. Six days, every session. Each day builds on the one before. Everyone is welcome: members, first-timers, friends and family. Bring someone with you.

What should I bring?

Yourself first, and someone with you. Then your Bible, notebook and pen, a warm layer for the early-morning sessions, and your offering.
